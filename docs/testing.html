How the riding works

Push the stick forward and the nose bites for speed; pull it back and the nose lifts and you turn tighter. The button is a pump: hold to crouch, release to drive. Each pump builds speed, which is how you get going across the flat parts.

Hard turns tire you out, and a tired surfer fights the board instead of riding it — so a long ride is about choosing which turns are worth it. Turns and carves score. Airs do not score yet, though the board will leave the water if the speed and the lip are there.

Five boards, easiest to hardest. Start on the foamie: floaty and hard to sink. The shortboard will punish a lazy turn.
