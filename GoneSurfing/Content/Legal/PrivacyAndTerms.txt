GONE SURFING - PRIVACY & TERMS

Last updated: 2026-09-17


PRIVACY

Gone Surfing runs entirely on your phone.

What it stores: your best rides, the board you picked and your settings, in the app's own
storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What it sends: nothing. The game has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network
features. It never reads your contacts, location, photos or anything else on the device.

What it asks for: no permissions beyond what the app store installs it with.

If a later version changes any of this, this page and the store listing will say so before it
does.
